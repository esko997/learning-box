Ribbon Flight

Drag anywhere in the sky to fly. Your ribbon tips shoot automatically! Dodge the little flyers and protect your blue face.

♥ ♥ ♥Score 0
Ribbon Flight needs a browser with canvas support.
Ready to fly?An endless sky of little flyers awaits.

Tap Start flying when you are ready.

Keyboard: click the sky, then use arrows or WASD. P pauses. You have three hearts. The ribbon arms are safe to touch; only the blue face can be bumped. Flyers that get past you do not cost a heart. Turn your tablet sideways for a bigger sky.
